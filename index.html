A note left open on the desk

I am in a season of making my inner life less private.

I am writing a fiction manuscript, developing an independent short film and continuing my MA in English Literature. My research keeps pulling me toward Dalit writing, image-making, authorship and the politics of who gets to tell a story.

Professionally, I am interested in the point where cultural insight becomes an idea, and where that idea survives production without losing its soul. Personally, I am trying to document the things I usually assume I will remember.

04 / The life index

How the shelves got here

A biography told through turning points, not job titles.

2018—21

Finding a public voice

Journalism school, service learning, classrooms, slogans, reporting and the first serious attempts to understand what a story can do in the world.

2019

Reporting through Cyclone Fani

Ground reporting in Odisha made neat narrative arcs feel dishonest. Real life rarely resolves before the deadline.

Then

Learning to turn observation into ideas

At Schbang, culture, copy, scripts and campaigns became a daily practice. #BeTheChangeForTB showed what happens when an idea earns participation.

Later

Entering the invisible side of film

Netflix VFX production revealed the thousands of decisions behind a believable frame—from The Royals to more than 800 shots on Mismatched S3.

Now

Returning to authorship

Writing a manuscript, developing an independent film and building a practice where strategy, literature, production and personal curiosity can coexist.

05 / About this place

A library is more honest than a portfolio.

A portfolio asks, “What have you accomplished?” A library asks, “What have you paid attention to?” I prefer the second question.

This archive holds published writing and unfinished notes, films I made and films that remade me, work memories, screenshots, reading lists, visual experiments, conversations with ideas and evidence of changing my mind.

Some shelves will be orderly. Some will be crowded. Some will stay empty until I become the person who can fill them.
