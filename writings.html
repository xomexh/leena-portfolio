01 Essay Localisation is not translation A note on language, Odisha, and why cultural specificity is not a decorative layer. Read

The quickest way to flatten a place is to treat its language like a technical problem. Translation asks whether the sentence survived. Localisation asks whether the person on the other side recognised themselves.

This is a starting point for a longer essay about language, culture, and the work of seeing a place clearly.

02 Production note The invisible labour behind an impossible frame What hundreds of VFX shots taught me about disciplined, largely unseen decisions. Read

A finished frame hides its scaffolding. Schedules, versions, handovers, compromises, late fixes, and questions that sound tiny until an entire shot depends on them.

Production is not separate from the creative act. It is the practice that gives a difficult idea a chance to remain intact.

03 Research notebook Who gets to narrate, and who is made into material? Questions from reading Dalit literature while researching fiction and film. Read

Research changes when the subject is not simply a subject. It becomes a question of position: who is looking, who is speaking, and what the work takes without returning.

This notebook holds questions, contradictions, and changes in the project's ethical position, not only conclusions.
